// Our Model

We Only Win
When You Win.

The traditional consulting model charges you regardless of results. We rejected that entirely. Jolt operates on a pure gain-share basis: we have skin in the game on every engagement.

Bring us your renewal, your new purchase, your vendor quote. We scan it, assess the opportunity, and if we see room to negotiate, we get to work. You pay nothing unless we deliver savings.

When we do save you money, we take 25% of the gross savings — once. You keep the remaining 75%, every year the deal renews. The math almost always works overwhelmingly in your favor.

✓
Zero financial risk. If Jolt doesn't save you money, you pay nothing. No hidden fees, no retainers, no "consulting hours."
